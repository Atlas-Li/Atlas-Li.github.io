I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Computing Sciences at the University of Hartford .

I earned my Ph.D. in Computer Science from Clarkson University and M.S. in Computational Sciences from Marquette University.

My research focuses on:
  • machine learning
  • deep learning
  • 2D/3D computer vision
  • human robot interaction
  • virtual reality
My recent work includes developing advanced deep learning models for VR-based biometric authentication and human motion forecasting, as well as building large-scale multi-modal datasets for the human robot interaction community.
